# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

NVO987 – Culture Visuelle Moderne et Contemporaine is committed to maintaining a respectful, professional, inclusive, and constructive environment.

All participants, contributors, researchers, collaborators, and visitors are expected to communicate respectfully and professionally.

## Expected Behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and professionalism.
- Welcome constructive discussion and differing viewpoints.
- Focus criticism on ideas, documentation, research, and technical matters.
- Respect privacy and personal boundaries.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and abusive behaviour.
- Provide accurate information whenever possible.
- Respect intellectual property and attribution requirements.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- Harassment or threats.
- Discriminatory or hateful conduct.
- Personal attacks.
- Deliberate publication of private information.
- Impersonation or fraudulent representation.
- Malicious disruption of services.
- Spam or abusive automated activity.
- Deliberate submission of misleading information.

## Reporting

Concerns regarding behaviour or violations may be reported to:

contact@nvo987.fr

Please provide sufficient information to allow the matter to be reviewed.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions associated with NVO987 and its digital properties, repositories, publications, and collaborative activities.

## Enforcement

Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as appropriately as possible while respecting privacy and applicable law.
